My Top 3 Favorite Artists

CORTIS, AC/DC, Steve Lacy

    1. CORTIS

CORTIS is a K-Pop boy group under BIGHIT consisting of 5 members. They've experimented with various genres such as hip-hop, trap, rock, alternative, pop, and many more. They recently won a Daesang for 'Record of the Year'. Their music embodies the experiences teenagers usually go through.

Listen to CORTIS here.

    2. AC/DC

AC/DC is an Australian, heavy metal, and rock band. After one of the core members, Malcolm Young, was diagnosed with dementia, his nephew, Stevie Young, now plays with the band. Two of their most noticable and iconic songs are "Highway to Hell" and "Back in Black", each from their own respective albums.

Listen to AC/DC here.

    3. Steve Lacy

Steve Lacy is an American singer-songwriter and producer. He helped in producing other songs and albums of famous celebrities like Kendrick Lamar, Tyler the Creator, Brent Faiyaz, and more. He recorded all his Grammy-nominated on his Iphone and Ipod.
